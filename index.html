02

The insight

Let AI reason. Never let it calculate.

Language models reason well, and they also invent numbers. Astrea splits the work: models decide what to look at and explain the answer; deterministic engines produce every figure.

Reasoning plane · LLM agents

  • Supervisor and specialist agents handle intent, routing, causal data selection and synthesis
  • Reasons over topology and causality; narrates, cites and routes work to the solvers
  • Never computes a number — no forecast math, no value into a bid, no raw timeseries

Computation plane · deterministic engines

  • Forecasting service: ARIMA/SARIMAX baseline and a Temporal Fusion Transformer producing P10/P50/P90
  • Optimization service: Gurobi mixed-integer programming for battery dispatch and market bids
  • Versioned and sandboxed: every value carries a run ID and SHA-256 hash, fully reproducible

calls · validated inputs  →  numbers + variable importance

Analyst in the loop

A person verifies every causal link. Model swaps are gated on a 50-plus scenario regression suite, with signed validation per upgrade.

03

How Astrea works in time

Two weeks forward to see it. One week back to learn.

The value sits in the few days that break the pattern. Astrea picks them up two weeks out, sharpens their timing every day, and grades every call once it has settled.

D+14 detect

See it coming

A heat wave, wind lull, outage or new large load first appears as a probability, wide and uncertain.

D+9–5 refine

Narrow the window

Each daily refresh tightens when it lands, how deep and how long. Hold capacity, move maintenance, hedge.

D+1 lock

A dated shape

Bids and dispatch for tomorrow. Out to D+5, state of charge or the utility's peak plan.

D−7 grade

Score every call

Each miss split into four causes: forecast, optimizer, market access, availability. Each has a different fix.

Stated plainly

Fourteen days forward decide what the asset or the utility does. Seven days back decide what the forecast is worth. Scored on lead time, timing convergence, shift capture and false alarms.
